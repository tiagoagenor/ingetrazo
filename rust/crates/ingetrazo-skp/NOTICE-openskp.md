# openskp

This crate is a port of the `.skp` reader of **openskp**
(https://github.com/iamahsanmehmood/openskp, `packages/python`: `openskp/_core.py`,
`legacy.py`, `model.py`, `vff.py`, `parser.py`), as pinned in IngeTrazo's
`requirements.txt` at commit 291700bc213655a00461838de71e5f206311e619, plus the
two fixes IngeTrazo applied at run time (`formats/openskp_compat.py`).
